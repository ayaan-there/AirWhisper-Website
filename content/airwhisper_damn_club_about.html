AirWhisper / About us Independent communication · Est. together 01 — Our story

A small team. A deeply human problem.

We built for the moments when words don't come.

AirWhisper began with one question: what if someone who couldn't speak could still express what they wanted? One year of experiments led us to a simple idea — turn the movement of a hand into a message, right from the wrist.

NOT A STRAIGHT LINE. NEVER WAS.
01 / THE QUESTION

Start with listening.

We began with the person, not the device — and the need to communicate without relying on speech.

02 / THE DATA

Make what is missing.

When we couldn't find a dataset for our gestures, we had to create one and learn from it ourselves.

03 / THE BUILD

Test. Learn. Rebuild.

We explored wrist motion, recognition models and on-device processing until the pieces began to connect.

04 / THE PURPOSE

Make it personal.

AirWhisper is designed around a simple belief: communication should feel immediate, private and independent.

Not built to speak for someone.
Built to help them speak for themselves.
